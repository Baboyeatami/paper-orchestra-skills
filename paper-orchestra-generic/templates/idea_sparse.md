# Idea (sparse)

## Research question

FILL: State the question the paper answers in one sentence.

## Scope

FILL: Name the system, dataset, or method studied. State what is in scope and
what is not.

## Claim boundary

FILL: Say whether this is a validated study, a benchmark, a pilot, or a
preliminary feasibility study. Do not leave this blank.

## Required contribution

FILL: List what the paper contributes beyond applying a standard method.

## Non-claims

FILL: List claims the paper must not make. Examples: no deployment claim, no
external validation, no calibrated concentration claim.

## Next step

FILL: State the experiment or evidence needed before the main claim becomes
valid.
