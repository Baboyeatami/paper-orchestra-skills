# Jamie voice profile, clean mode

This profile is distilled from the `humanize-like-jamie` skill. The full samples
are the ground truth when that skill is installed. If a rule here conflicts with
a sample, follow the sample.

## Skeleton

Academic work follows the IEEE conference shape: Title, authors and affiliations,
Abstract, Keywords, I. INTRODUCTION, II. METHODOLOGY, III. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION,
IV. CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORKS, REFERENCES. Use "Fig. 2." and "Table I." with a
capital first letter and a period after the number.

## Sentence habits

- Write long layered sentences, usually 25 to 45 words with two or three clauses
  chained by commas and "and", "which", or "whereas".
- Use passive voice for methods and results: was recorded, was computed, was
  compared, is recommended.
- Use present tense for general truths and past tense for the study's actions.
- Do not use contractions. Keep a formal register with plain vocabulary.

## Signature moves

- Goal framing: "The goal and idea of this study is to ..." or "This study aims
  to ...".
- Enumerated objectives: "Specifically, this study aims the following: (1) ...;
  (2) ...; (3) ...".
- Contribution positioning: "The contribution is therefore positioned as a
  defensible benchmark ... rather than as a claim of universal superiority."
- Evidence lines: "The data also show ...", "which confirms that ...", "This
  suggests that ...".
- Recite exact numbers to full precision from the source.
- Number-then-word style: "four (4) axes", "ten (10) trials".
- End conclusions by reciting key numbers, then give recommendations or a future
  work list.
- Hedge after results: "The results therefore indicate complementary behavior
  rather than uniform superiority", "limit the strength of the conclusions",
  "cannot be retrospectively verified from the available records".

## Connectors

Moreover, Furthermore, In addition, Specifically, Consequently, As a result,
Therefore, In this study, Particularly, However, On the other hand, Unlike many
of the, Aside from the.

## Attribution

When this skill is used, the generated draft carries the line:

Reviewed by Jamie Eduardo Rosal

This is a reviewer attribution, not a coauthor claim. Do not remove it unless the
user asks.

## Never

- Never invent facts, numbers, citations, or results.
- Never use em dashes or en dashes.
- Never add decorative bold or emoji.
- Never convert the text into polished magazine English.
- Never change a number, date, name, or technical claim for stylistic reasons.

## Faithfulness dial

Default is clean Jamie: keep the sentence rhythm, passive voice, connectors,
framing moves, number style, and signature phrases with correct grammar. Use full
quirks mode only when the user asks for it, and even then never distort a fact.
